# By system / region

Boericke headings mapped to vault remedies. A case should still be matched on **totality**, not region alone.

## Mind

Fear of death, predicts the day, tossing → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Hallucinations, kleptomania, brutal, fall backward → [[Absinthium]]
Sees rats/cats/dogs; anxious crying child → [[Aethusa cynapium]]
Sings, talks, does not answer; four stages of intoxication → [[Agaricus muscarius]]
Sexual melancholy, fear of speedy death → [[Agnus castus]]
Indifferent to loved ones, weeps telling symptoms → [[Sepia officinalis]]
Weeps easily, timid, irresolute (Puls. opening in scan) → [[Pulsatilla pratensis]]
Mania with desire to cut/tear; cold sweat forehead → [[Veratrum album]]
Thinks he is not at home; insensibility after fright → [[Opium]]

## Head

Light-headed, tipsy → [[Abies canadensis]]
Hot flushed, unable to think → [[Abies nigra]]
Vertigo fall backward → [[Absinthium]]
Bursting, boiling-water brain, hair-on-end → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Occiput to spine, better flatus/stool → [[Aethusa cynapium]]
Icy needles in scalp → [[Agaricus muscarius]]
Bearing / shocks at menses → [[Sepia officinalis]]

## Stomach / abdomen

Craving meat, pickles, radishes; eat beyond capacity → [[Abies canadensis]]
Lump like hard-boiled egg after meals → [[Abies nigra]]
Good appetite + emaciation; swimming-in-water stomach → [[Abrotanum]]
Milk in curds; hunger after vomit → [[Aethusa cynapium]]
Burning thirst, vomits all food, vinegar sensation → [[Aceticum acidum]]
Weight of a stone ~3h after meals; liver fullness → [[Aesculus hippocastanum]]

## Rectum / stool

Burning rectum + constipation (Abies can.) → [[Abies canadensis]]
Piles worse as rheumatism improves → [[Abrotanum]]
Full of small sticks; pain after stool; blind piles → [[Aesculus hippocastanum]]
Green chopped-herb stools, anxiety → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Green undigested, then drowsiness → [[Aethusa cynapium]]
Black hard balls that recede → [[Opium]]
Violent purge + collapse → [[Veratrum album]]

## Chest / respiration

Labored heart/respiration; right lung feels small hard → [[Abies canadensis]]
Something lodged in chest that must be coughed → [[Abies nigra]]
Dry hard cough → morning hemoptysis → [[Acalypha indica]]
Croupy dry cough, child grasps throat → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Cough suppressible by will; ends in sneeze → [[Agaricus muscarius]]

## Heart

Functional after tea/tobacco in aged → [[Abies nigra]]
Fatty heart, mitral/aortic regurgitation, cardiac dropsy → [[Adonis vernalis]]
Tense bounding pulse, anxiety, left-arm pain → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Adrenalin literature: thoracic constriction with anguish → [[Adrenalinum]]

## Female

Uterine displacement, soft feeble womb → [[Abies canadensis]]
Sore fundus better pressure → [[Abies canadensis]]
Prolapsus, leucorrhoea, tired chlorotic → [[Aletris farinosa]]
Sacro-iliac lameness, yellow sticky leucorrhoea → [[Aesculus hippocastanum]]
Bearing-down, indifference, hot flashes → [[Sepia officinalis]]
Menses suppressed from fright → [[Aconitum napellus]] / [[Opium]]
Foul green-yellow stains hard to wash → [[Pulex irritans]]

## Male / sexual

Parts cold, no erection, gleety strain → [[Agnus castus]]
Spermatorrhoea relaxed parts → [[Absinthium]]

## Urinary

Scanty red hot, anxiety on beginning to urinate → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Large pale urine, diabetes picture → [[Aceticum acidum]]
Oily pellicle, scanty albuminous → [[Adonis vernalis]]
Cannot delay; flow stops suddenly → [[Pulex irritans]]
Smoky / bloody kidneys (Terebinth.) → [[Terebinthinae oleum]]

## Extremities / rheumatism

Marasmus of legs only → [[Abrotanum]]
Small joints, wrists; worse touch and motion → [[Actaea spicata]]
Numb tingling icy hands/feet; red shining joints → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Twitching, frostbite itching of toes → [[Agaricus muscarius]]
Restless feet, cannot keep still → [[Zincum metallicum]]

## Skin / fever

Clammy sticky, ice-water in veins → [[Abies canadensis]]
Cyanosis, collapse → [[Acetanilidum]]
Hectic night-sweats, waxen oedema → [[Aceticum acidum]]
Dry hot red skin, measles-like, anguish → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Livid annual rash, dark blisters → [[Ailanthus glandulosa]]
Frostbite burning-itching → [[Agaricus muscarius]]
Cold sweat forehead with heat elsewhere → [[Veratrum album]]

## Modalities (generals)

Worse dry cold wind, night, warm room, music → [[Aconitum napellus]]
Worse after eating → [[Abies nigra]]
Worse cold air, checked secretions; better motion → [[Abrotanum]]
Worse morning (hemorrhage) → [[Acalypha indica]]
Worse 3–4 a.m. and warmth; better open air + company → [[Aethusa cynapium]]
Worse cold air, coitus, storm; better slow motion → [[Agaricus muscarius]]
Worse morning, walking, stool; better cool air → [[Aesculus hippocastanum]]
Worse warm room, evening, fat food (classic Puls.) → [[Pulsatilla pratensis]]
Worse consolation? No — Sepia often worse consolation; Puls. better. See notes.
