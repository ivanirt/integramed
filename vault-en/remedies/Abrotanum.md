# Abrotanum

## Keynotes

- **Marasmus of lower extremities** with **good appetite**.
- **Metastasis**. Rheumatism after checked diarrhea. Ill effects of suppressed conditions, gouty subjects.
- Tuberculous peritonitis; exudative pleurisy. Pressing remains after chest operations for hydrothorax/empyema.
- Hemorrhoids **aggravate when rheumatism improves**.
- Nosebleed and hydrocele in boys. Great weakness after influenza (Kali-p.).

## Mind / face

Cross, irritable, anxious, depressed. Wrinkled cold dry pale face; blue rings; comedones with emaciation.

## GI

Food undigested despite appetite. Night cutting gnawing stomach; feels swimming in water, cold. Alternate diarrhea/constipation. Bloody piles; ascarides; oozing umbilicus.

## Extremities

Legs greatly emaciated. Joints stiff. Painful contraction of limbs (Am-m.). Neck too weak to hold head up.
