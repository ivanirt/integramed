# Acalypha indica

Alimentary + respiratory. Incipient phthisis: hard racking cough, bloody expectoration, arterial hemorrhage, **no febrile disturbance**. Weak morning, stronger by day. Progressive emaciation. Pathological hemorrhages with **morning aggravation**.

Chest: dry hard cough then hemoptysis; worse morning and night. Blood **bright red, not profuse in morning**; **dark clotted in afternoon**.

Abdomen: spluttering diarrhea with noisy flatus, tenesmus. Rectal hemorrhage worse morning.
