# Aceticum acidum

Profound anemia with dropsy, debility, fainting, dyspnea, weak heart, vomiting, profuse urine and sweat. Hemorrhage from any part. Pale lean lax flabby. Wasting. Historical notes on liquefying albuminous deposits, epithelial cancer (Owens) — record only, not a treatment claim.

Mind: irritable, worried about business.

Stomach: intense burning thirst; cold drinks distress; vomits every food; as if taken a lot of vinegar.

Urine: large pale quantities; diabetes picture with thirst (Ph-ac.).

Female: hemorrhages after labor; impoverished bluish sour milk; anemia of nursing mothers.

Fever: hectic drenching night-sweats; red spot left cheek; **no thirst in fever**.
