# Aesculus hippocastanum

## Keynotes

Lower bowel: engorged hemorrhoidal veins, **characteristic backache**, **absence of actual constipation**. Much pain, little bleeding. General venous stasis; purple varices; everything slowed. Liver/portal torpor. Back aches and **gives out**. Throat with hemorrhoidal conditions.

## Rectum

Dry aching. Feels **full of small sticks**. Anus raw. Pain after stool with prolapse. Blind and bleeding piles; sharp shooting **up the back**; worse climacteric. Large hard dry stools. Mucosa swollen obstructs passage.

## Back / female

Sacro-iliac / sacral-hip backache worse walking or stooping. Feet turn under. Leucorrhoea with lameness across sacro-iliac; dark yellow sticky corroding; worse after menses.

## Differentiate

[[Sepia officinalis]] = pelvic bearing-down + mental indifference. Aesculus = rectal sticks + sacral “gives out,” mind more irritable/depressed than indifferent.
