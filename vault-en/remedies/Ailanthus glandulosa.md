# Ailanthus glandulosa

Disorganizes blood (Boericke language): low fevers, low eruptive diseases, diphtheria, follicular tonsillitis, strep, hemorrhagic diathesis. Skin livid/purplish; face dark as mahogany; sordes; throat swollen purple; semi-conscious; weak pulse; torpor. Remarkably like **malignant scarlatina**. Adynamia. Mucosa hemorrhagic/ulcerative (Lach., Ars.).

Throat: oedematous dusky red; pain on swallow to ears; hoarse croupy voice.

Skin: miliary livid rash returning annually; large blisters dark serum; patchy livid eruption disappearing on pressure; Raynaud listed.
