# Opium

Partial extract scan ~p. 500 (mid-remedy).

Painlessness where pain is expected (classic, complete from full article). Thinks he is not at home. Unable to understand his sufferings. Delirious talking with wide-open eyes.

Head: lightness in old people; weight in occiput (Gels.); bursting; complete insensibility; paralysis of brain.

Face: red bloated dark suffused; looks intoxicated (Bapt., Lach.); hanging lower jaw.

Stool: obstinate constipation, no desire; round hard **black balls**; faeces protrude and recede (Thuj., Sil.). Involuntary black offensive frothy stools.

Urine: retained or involuntary after fright.

Female: menses suppressed from fright.

## vs Aconite

Both after fright. Aconite = anxious tossing + intolerant pain + dry heat. Opium = stupid insensibility, staring, lack of pain, dark bloated face.
