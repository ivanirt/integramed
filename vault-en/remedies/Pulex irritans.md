# Pulex irritans

Marked urinary and female symptoms.

Cannot retain urine; must attend without delay. Flow stops suddenly then pain. Foul urine. Irritable bladder before menses.

Leucorrhoea profuse foul staining greenish yellow; stains of menses/leucorrhoea **very hard to wash out**. Intense burning in vagina. Menses delayed. Backache (Ox-ac.).

Skin emits foul odor; prickly itching.
