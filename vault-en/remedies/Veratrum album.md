# Veratrum album

Scan p. 680:

Perfect picture of **collapse**: extreme coldness, blueness, weakness. Post-operative shock: cold sweat on forehead, pale face, rapid feeble pulse. **Cold perspiration on the forehead** with nearly all complaints. Vomiting, purging, cramps. Profuse violent retching most characteristic. Surgical shock. Excessive dryness of mucosa. Coprophagia; violent mania alternates with silence.

## Mind

Melancholy with stupor and mania. Sits stupid; sullen indifference. Frenzy, shrieks, curses. Puerperal mania. Aimless wandering. Delusions of impending misfortune. Desire to cut and tear (Tarent.). Cursing howling all night.

## Particulars

Sensation of a lump of ice on vertex. Face sunken, icy tip of nose, pointed nose, pale blue collapsed. Neck too weak to hold head.
