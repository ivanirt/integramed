# Aconitum napellus

See [[relationships/GRAPH#Aconitum-napellus]] · [[indexes/KEYNOTES]]

## Keynotes (Boericke)

- State of **fear, anxiety, anguish**; physical and mental **restlessness**; fright.
- Acute, sudden, violent invasion with fever.
- Does not want to be touched.
- Complaints from **dry cold weather**, draught, checked perspiration; also very hot weather (GI).
- First remedy in inflammations / inflammatory fevers — **beginning only**, before exudation / tissue change.
- Action brief; no periodicity; functional disturbance.

## Mind

Great fear and worry with every ailment. Predicts the day of death. Fears future, crowds, crossing streets. Tossing. Pains intolerable. Music unbearable.

## Selected particulars

- Head: bursting, boiling-water sensation; vertigo on rising.
- Eyes: dry hot as if sand; after dry cold winds / snow glare.
- Face: one cheek red, other pale on rising becomes deathly pale.
- Stomach: intense thirst for cold water; drinks, vomits, declares he will die.
- Stool: small frequent, green like chopped herbs; choleraic with anxiety.
- Urine: scanty, red, hot; anxiety on beginning to urinate.
- Chest: dry croupy cough; child grasps throat; worse after midnight.
- Heart: full hard bounding pulse; palpitation with tingling fingers.
- Fever: thirst + restlessness always; cold waves; dry heat red face.

## Agent tips

Do not continue after pathology localizes. Contrast [[Veratrum album]] (collapse + cold sweat forehead) and [[Aethusa cynapium]] (milk, child sleeps after vomit).
