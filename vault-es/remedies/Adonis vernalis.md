# Adonis vernalis

Heart medicine after rheumatism, influenza, or Bright’s, when myocardium is in fatty degeneration: regulates pulse, increases contraction power, increases urine. Cardiac dropsy. Low vitality, slow weak pulse. Hydrothorax, ascites, anasarca.

Heart: mitral/aortic regurgitation; rheumatic endocarditis (Kalmia); praecordial pain; cardiac asthma (Quebracho); pulse rapid irregular.

Urine: oily pellicle; scanty albuminous.
