# Per-domain interview

## Homeopathy

Order — chief complaint, sensation, location, radiation, time, modalities (worse/better), concomitants, mind/fear, etiology (exposure, fright), discharge character.

Score — strange-rare-peculiar first, then generals, then particulars. Use compare/complement/antidote from GRAPH.

## Peptides

Order — goal, current diagnoses and meds, pregnancy, cancer history, glucose issues, legal access, evidence-tier preference.

Score — goal match, evidence tier (label > late trial > research peptide > vendor bioregulator), contraindications as veto. No fused stack unless user already under care.

## Herbal

Order — tissue state, heat/cold dry/damp, digestion, sleep, cycle, current pharmaceuticals.

Score — matching tissue action + safety vs drugs. Name binomial + part used.

## TCM

Order — cold/heat, interior/exterior, deficiency/excess, yin/yang; then zang-fu; tongue/pulse if offered.

Score — pattern first, formula second. Do not list 12 raw herbs as a DIY pharmacy.

## Ayurveda

Order — prakriti vs vikriti, agni, ama, season, sleep, elimination.

Score — dosha-reducing measures + classical category. Flag heavy metals in rasa shastra talk.
