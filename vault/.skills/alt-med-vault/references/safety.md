# Safety language

Use this wording, adapted to the domain.

## Required footer on every recommendation

This is a map of traditional or research literature in the vault, not a medical diagnosis, prescription, or instruction to self-administer. Confirm with a licensed clinician. Do not stop prescribed drugs because of a vault match.

## Stop and refer (any domain)

- Chest pain, stroke signs, difficulty breathing, anaphylaxis
- Suicidal intent or active self-harm — give local crisis resources, do not continue matching
- Pregnancy with bleeding, ectopic-pain picture, pre-eclampsia signs
- Febrile infant, immunocompromised + fever, rapidly spreading infection
- Severe hypoglycemia, DKA picture, adrenal crisis picture

## Domain extras

- Homeopathy — do not delay emergency care for “Aconite for panic.”
- Peptides — no DIY reconstitution; incretins only via prescriber; flag cancer history on GH/IGF talk.
- Herbal — drug–herb CYP and anticoagulant interactions before naming a tea as “safe.”
- TCM / Ayurveda — pattern language is not a substitute for imaging or labs the user already needs.
