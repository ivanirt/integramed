# Abies canadensis

## Keynotes

- Mucous membranes; **gastric** symptoms most marked; catarrhal stomach.
- Peculiar **cravings** and **chilly** sensations; women with **uterine displacement** from defective nutrition.
- Wants to lie down; skin cold clammy; very faint.
- Right lung and liver feel small and hard.

## Mind / head

Light-headed, tipsy. Irritable.

## Stomach

Canine hunger with torpid liver. Gnawing faint epigastrium. Craves meat, pickles, radishes, turnips, artichokes, coarse food. Eats beyond digestive capacity. Burning distention with palpitation. Flatulence disturbs heart.

## Female

Uterine displacements. Sore fundus **better pressure**. Thinks womb soft and feeble. Prostration.

## Fever

Blood as ice-water (Acon.). Chills down back. Cold water between shoulders (Am-m.). Clammy sticky skin. Night-sweat (China).
