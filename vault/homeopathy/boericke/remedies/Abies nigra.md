# Abies nigra

## Keynotes

Long-acting when the **characteristic stomach** symptoms are present. Dyspepsia of the **aged** with functional heart symptoms; after tea or tobacco. Constipation. Pain in external meatus.

## Pathognomonic sensation

Pain in stomach **always after eating**. Lump as if a **hard-boiled egg** lodged at cardiac end. Continual constriction above the pit, as if knotted up.

## Other

Appetite lost in morning, craving noon and night. Offensive breath. Chest as if something lodged that must be coughed; lungs compressed; worse lying down; waterbrash after cough. Wakeful hungry nights. Alternate heat and cold; intermittent with stomach pain.
