# Absinthium

Perfect picture of **epileptiform seizure**. Nervous tremors precede. Sudden severe giddiness, delirium with hallucinations, loss of consciousness. Hysterical and infantile spasms. Mushroom poisoning (listed). Chorea, tremor. Nervous sleepless children.

Mind: frightful visions, kleptomania, loss of memory, brutal, wants nothing to do with anybody.

Head: vertigo **fall backward**; wants head low; unequally dilated pupils; blue face; occipital headache (Gels., Pic-ac.).

Mouth: jaws fixed; bites tongue; tongue feels swollen too large.

Dose and compares as frontmatter.
