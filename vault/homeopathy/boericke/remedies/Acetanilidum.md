# Acetanilidum

Depresses heart, respiration, blood pressure; lowers temperature. Cyanosis and collapse. Increased susceptibility to cold. Destroys RBCs; pallor. Enlarged-head sensation, fainting, moral depravity. Weak irregular heart, blue mucous membranes, albuminuria, ankle oedema.
