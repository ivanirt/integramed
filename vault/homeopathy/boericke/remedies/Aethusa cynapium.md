# Aethusa cynapium

## Keynotes

Brain/nervous symptoms tied to GI. Children in dentition / summer complaint: **cannot digest milk**, poor circulation. Symptoms set in with violence. Anguish, crying, uneasiness.

## Mind

Restless anxious crying. Sees rats, cats, dogs. Unconscious, delirious. Brain-fag. Idiocy may alternate with furor.

## Stomach / stool

**Intolerance of milk** — vomited as soon as swallowed or in **large curds**. Hungry after vomiting. Violent white frothy vomit. Nausea at sight of food. Then **sleepiness**. Undigested thin **greenish** stool with tenesmus then exhaustion. Cholera infantum: cold clammy stupid, staring dilated pupils.

## Other

Head bound in a vise; occiput down spine better lying/pressure and after flatus/stool. Linea nasalis marked. Fingers/thumbs clenched. Must be covered during sweat.
