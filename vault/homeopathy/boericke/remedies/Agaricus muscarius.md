# Agaricus muscarius

## Keynotes

Muscarin toxicology delayed 12–14h (historical). Intoxicant: more vertigo/delirium than alcohol, then sopor. **Jerking, twitching, trembling, itching.** Twitching **ceases during sleep**. Icy needles. Symptoms **diagonal** (right arm + left leg). Pains with cold, numbness, tingling. Incipient phthisis / tubercular diathesis mentioned.

## Mind — four proving phases

1. Cheerfulness, courage, loquacity  
2. Incoherent talking; size distortion (small hole = chasm)  
3. Furious delirium, wants to injure himself  
4. Depression, languor, indifference  

Sings, talks, does not answer. Not Belladonna’s active congestion — nervous excitement of DT / febrile delirium.

## Skin / extremities

Burning itching redness swelling **as from frostbites**. Chilblains. Itching of toes as if frozen. Uncertain gait, trembling.

## Cough

Violent cough suppressible by effort of will; spasmodic after falling asleep; little mucus balls; cough ends in sneeze.
