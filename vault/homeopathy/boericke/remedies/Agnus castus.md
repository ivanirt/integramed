# Agnus castus

Lowers sexual vitality with mental depression and loss of nervous energy — both sexes, stronger in men. Premature old age from abuse of sexual power. History of repeated gonorrhea. Sprains. Gnawing itching especially eyes. Tachycardia from tobacco in neurotic young men.

Mind: sexual melancholy; fear of death; impression of speedy death; absent-minded; illusion of smell (herrings, musk).

Male: no erections; parts cold relaxed; desire gone (Sel., Con., Sabal.); scanty emission without ejaculation; loss of prostatic fluid on straining; testicles cold swollen hard painful.

Female: scanty menses; abhorrence of intercourse; agalactia with sadness; yellow staining transparent leucorrhoea.
