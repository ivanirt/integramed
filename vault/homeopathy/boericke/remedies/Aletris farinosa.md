# Aletris farinosa

Anemic **relaxed** female organism. Tired all the time. Prolapsus, leucorrhoea, rectal distress. Marked anemia. Chlorotic girls and pregnant women.

Mind: power and energy weakened; cannot concentrate; fainting with vertigo.

Mouth: much frothy saliva.
