# Pulsatilla pratensis

Scan p. 550 begins: “The weather-cock among remedies. The disposition and mental state are the chief guiding symptoms.”

## School keynotes used with that opening

Changeable symptoms. Thirstless with dry mouth. Weeps easily, timid, irresolute, wants sympathy. Thick bland yellow-green discharges. Late scanty changeable menses. Worse heat, better open air. Not for the agent to invent particulars beyond Boericke; when expanding, copy remaining Boericke sections from a complete page set.

## Differentiate

vs [[Sepia officinalis]]: Puls. seeks consolation and company; Sepia is indifferent, irritable, wants to be alone yet dreads it.
vs [[Aconitum napellus]]: Acon. is sudden terror + thirst; Puls. is mild, thirstless, changeable.
