# Sepia officinalis

From scan p. 600:

Acts on portal system, venous congestion. Stasis → ptosis of viscera, weariness, misery. Weakness, yellow complexion, **bearing-down**, especially women. Pains to back; chills easily. Tendency to abortion. Hot flashes at menopause with weakness and sweat. **Upward** tendency of symptoms. Easy fainting. “Ball” sensation in inner parts. Best on brunettes. All pains from below up. Uterine polychrest. Feels cold even in warm room. Pulsating cerebellar headache.

## Mind

Indifferent to those loved best. Averse to occupation and family. Irritable, easily offended. Dreads to be alone. Very sad. **Weeps when telling symptoms**. Miserly. Anxious toward evening; indolent.

## Head

Vertigo as if something rolling in head. Stinging from within out and upward, mostly left or forehead, with nausea; worse indoors and lying on painful side. Hair falls. Headache in shocks at menstrual nisus with scanty flow. Coldness of vertex.
