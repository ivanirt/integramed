# Terebinthinae oleum

Selective affinity for **bleeding mucous surfaces**. Tympanites + urinary symptoms marked. Kidney inflammation with dark passive fetid hemorrhages. Bright’s preceded by dropsy (Goullon). Drowsiness and strangury. Coma. Unbroken chilblains.

Head: dull band around head (Carb-ac.). Tongue dry red sore shining; burning tip, prominent papillae (Arg-n., Bell., Kali-bi., Nux-m.).
