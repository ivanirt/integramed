# Actaea spicata

Rheumatic remedy of **small joints**; tearing tingling. Wrist rheumatism. Pulsations over body, especially liver and renal region. Cardiovascular spasm.

Head: fearful, starts easily; coffee causes head congestion; bone-crushed left frontal eminence.

Extremities: wrist swollen red worse any motion; paralytic weakness of hands; sudden lassitude after talking or eating.
