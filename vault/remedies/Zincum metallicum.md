# Zincum metallicum

From scan pp. 698–700 (late article + related zinc salts).

Weak memory; sensitive to noise; averse to work and talk. Child repeats everything said to it (opening of article in many editions).

Spine: dull ache last dorsal / first lumbar worse sitting; burning along spine; nape weary from writing.

Extremities: lameness weakness twitching. **Feet in continued motion; cannot keep still.** Varices of legs. Formication as of bugs crawling, preventing sleep. Retrocession of eruptions. Convulsions with pale face.

Sleep: cries out; body jerks; somnambulism (Kali-p.).

Related salts in Boericke: Zinc. aceticum (night-watching), bromatum, oxydatum, sulphuricum (corneal opacities — historical), cyanatum.
